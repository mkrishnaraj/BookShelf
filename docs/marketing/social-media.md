### First Comment — Maker's Note

Hi Product Hunt,

I built Virtual Bookshelf because I got tired of my reading life being scattered across apps that don't talk to each other.

My physical shelf didn't know about my Kindle library. Goodreads tracked my reads but looked like it was designed in 2009. And there was nowhere that actually felt like *mine* — somewhere that reflected how much I care about the books I've collected.

So I built a 3D library.

A few things that took the most work and that I'm most proud of:

**The sizing algorithm.** Books are rendered with spine widths based on actual page counts and standard publishing dimensions. It sounds like a small detail. The first time you see a shelf where everything is proportioned correctly, it's weirdly satisfying.

**The camera scan.** Point your phone at any book cover or spine — or your entire physical bookshelf — and we'll identify every book. One photo can import 30 books. That was hard to get right.

**The public shelf.** Every user gets a shareable URL. It's designed to be beautiful in a screenshot. We think shareable shelves will be the growth loop that actually works for a reading app.

We're free to start. If you import your Goodreads library today, you'll have a full shelf in minutes.

Happy to answer anything — what would you add to your shelf first?

**Headline:**

**Body:**

Hi r/books,

I've been building something that I wanted to exist for a long time, and I figured this community would give me the most honest take.

It's called Virtual Bookshelf. It renders your personal book collection as a photorealistic 3D shelf — books sized by page count, spines visible, browseable.

The core problem I was trying to solve: my reading history was scattered across Goodreads (which I've used for years but find increasingly frustrating), Kindle, a few Google Play Books I bought on sale, and a bunch of physical books I'd never catalogued anywhere. There was no single place that felt like *my* library.

What it does:

- Imports from Goodreads CSV, Kindle, Google Play Books, Kobo, Apple Books, or manual entry
- Renders everything as a 3D shelf with accurate spine sizing
- Tracks reading progress, streaks, and gives you weekly stats
- Camera scan — point your phone at a cover or spine to add books
- Public shelf URL you can share (the point is to make your reading life visible and shareable)
- Book marketplace for buying and selling physical books between readers

It's free to start (50 books, one shelf). Paid plans start at $3.99/month for the full import experience.

I'm genuinely curious what the readers here think. What would make you actually switch away from Goodreads (if anything)? What am I missing?

Link: virtualbookshelf.app

Happy to answer questions.
