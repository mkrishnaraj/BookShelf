## FAQ SECTION

**Section headline:**
Questions readers ask before their first shelf.

---

**Q: What exactly is Virtual Bookshelf?**
A: It's a web app (and installable PWA) that lets you build a photorealistic 3D version of your personal library. You add books from any source, and we render them as an accurate 3D shelf — books sized by page count, spines visible, browseable. Think of it as the home your reading life has always deserved.

---

**Q: Is my reading data private?**
A: Yes. Your shelf is private by default — only you can see it. You choose when to make it public and exactly what's visible. We don't sell your reading data. Ever. Your library belongs to you.

---

**Q: Can I import my Goodreads library?**
A: Absolutely. Export your Goodreads data as a CSV (it takes about a minute), and we'll import your entire reading history — including read dates, ratings, and shelves. Collector and Bibliophile plans also support direct CSV import with automatic shelf mapping.

---

**Q: Does it work offline?**
A: Virtual Bookshelf is a Progressive Web App, which means you can install it to your phone's home screen. Your shelf is cached for offline viewing — great for browsing your collection without a signal. Adding and syncing new books requires an internet connection.

---

**Q: How does the camera scan work?**
A: Open the app on your phone, tap the camera icon, and point it at a book cover or spine. Our AI identifies the title and author, pulls in the full metadata (cover art, page count, genre, description), and shows you a confirmation screen. Tap confirm and it's on your shelf. Collector and Bibliophile plans can scan an entire physical shelf in one photo.

---

**Q: Can I buy and sell books through the app?**
A: Yes. Reader plan and above can browse the marketplace. Collector and Bibliophile plan users can list their physical books for sale. Buyers pay securely through the app; sellers receive weekly payouts via Stripe. The platform takes a small commission (15% on Reader, 12% on Collector, 10% on Bibliophile) to cover payment processing and hosting.
