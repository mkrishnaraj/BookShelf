### EMAIL 2 — Day 1

**Subject line:**
Your existing books deserve a better home.

**Preview text:**
Import your Goodreads or Kindle library in under 60 seconds.

---

**Email body:**

Hi [First Name],

You've probably got hundreds of books scattered across apps you barely open anymore.

Your Goodreads shelf with read dates going back years. Your Kindle with 200 purchases you keep meaning to reread. Maybe a Kobo, a Google Play library, some Apple Books you bought on a whim.

They all belong together. Here's how to bring them home.

**Import from Goodreads**
1. Go to goodreads.com → My Books → Import/Export → Export Library
2. Download the CSV file
3. In Virtual Bookshelf: Settings → Import → Upload CSV

That's it. Every book, every rating, every read date — all here.

**Import from Kindle**
1. Go to privacy.amazon.com → Request Your Data → Kindle Library
2. You'll get a JSON file within a few minutes
3. Upload it in Virtual Bookshelf: Settings → Import → Kindle

**Other sources we support:**
- Google Play Books (Google Takeout)
- Apple Books / iBooks
- Kobo
- EPUB and PDF uploads
- Camera scan (Reader plan and above)

The more you import, the more beautiful your shelf gets.

[Import your library now →]

Talk tomorrow,
